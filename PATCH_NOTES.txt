BRAIN STASH — MERGED PROJECT PATCH

APPLYING THE ZIP
- This archive contains the complete source project under `brain-stash/`. It does not include Git history; extract it over your existing project folder to preserve your local `.git/` directory.
- It intentionally omits `node_modules/`, generated `dist/`, and local `.env` files. Dependencies are restored with `npm ci`; Vercel builds `dist/` from source.
- Do not add or upload an `.env` file. Keep AI credentials in Vercel's Environment Variables.

VERCEL AI SETTINGS
Keep the existing `AI_API_KEY` and `AI_BASE_URL`. Add this variable:
  Name: AI_MODELS
  Value: gemini-2.5-flash,gemini-2.5-flash-lite
Do not use quotation marks or brackets. The comma separates the primary model from the fallback model. The function tries the listed models in order and retains support for the legacy `AI_MODEL` variable.

CHANGES INCLUDED
- Added comma-separated AI_MODELS parsing and sequential Gemini fallback.
- Rebus re-entry resumes the current day's saved puzzle state, including its solved state.
- Rebus XP rewards are protected by puzzle ID and persist in local storage; replaying a solved puzzle cannot mint its 20 XP reward again.
- Skipping moves to a fresh unsolved puzzle without exposing the answer or granting a solve reward.
- Rebus explanations are authored per puzzle; the validator checks all 54 entries for unique IDs, non-placeholder explanations, and required fields.
- Integrated The Court's 20 case files into Home and Play navigation, and added the verdict, ruling, explanation, and factor-analysis screen.
- Added guard rails for Deep Stash archive entries with missing or malformed quiz data, so the app shows a recovery message instead of trying to render an invalid quiz.
- Added AI teaching API/client modules and the Play screen dependencies required by the updated app.
